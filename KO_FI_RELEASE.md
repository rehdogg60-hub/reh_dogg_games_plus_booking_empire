# Ko-fi Release Copy

## Short Description

Reh Dogg Games Plus: Booking Empire is a free-to-play browser wrestling management game where you book feuds, build heat, manage contracts, run PPVs, and try not to get fired.

## Long Description

Step into the neon office of Reh Dogg Games Plus and become the head booker of a fictional wrestling company. In Booking Empire, every feud is a business decision. Choose wrestlers, create rivalries, book weekly TV segments, manage morale, protect championship prestige, handle rival promotions, and build enough hype to make your monthly big events profitable.

The game is built for browser play with no download required. It includes fictional wrestlers, title belts, contracts, rankings, rival companies, sponsors, merchandise, news stories, chaos events, audience analytics, creative team advice, Hall of Fame tracking, and finisher preview clips.

Free to play. Donations are appreciated and help support more Reh Dogg Games Plus projects.

## Feature List

- Arcade wrestling management gameplay
- 10 original fictional wrestlers
- Weekly TV booking and segment ratings

- Sponsors and merchandise

- Tag teams and tag championships

- Chaos events and backstage drama
- Hall of Fame and legacy tracking
- Finisher video previews
